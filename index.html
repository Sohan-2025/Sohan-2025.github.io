02 FIELD NOTES

About

I'm a second-year CSE student at VIT Vellore with a genuine interest in coding — not just writing it, but understanding why it actually works the way it does. This semester I've been spending a lot of my time on sensors and embedded systems, figuring out how hardware and software talk to each other.

I'd probably call myself a slow learner in one sense — I don't rush to the surface-level version of a concept, I take the time to actually understand it deeply before moving on, even if that means going slower than everyone else at first.
